Blue Axolotl Advancement Version 1.2.3
For Minecraft 26.1 and higher
Created by Diantina

Thanks for downloading and using my datapack.
This Pack adds 1 Advancement related to the Blue Axolotl. The chance to get a Blue Axolotl from breeding is 1/1200 (0,083%) and no matter whether the parents have the same or different colors.
